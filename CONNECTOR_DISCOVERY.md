# Deel Connector — Discovery

## Vendor Architecture
- **Provider:** Deel Inc.
- **Platform:** Global Compliance, EOR, Payroll & Contractor Management
- **API Standards:** REST OpenAPI 3.0
- **Standard Base URL:** `https://api.letsdeel.com/rest/v2`
- **API Auth:** Personal API Tokens or OAuth 2.0 Bearer tokens

## Resource Capabilities
1. **People & Contracts:** Full CRUD on `/rest/v2/people` and contract tracking.
2. **Global Payroll:** Visibility into scheduled and executed payroll runs via `/rest/v2/payroll/runs`.
3. **Organizations:** Department, team, and cost center management via `/rest/v2/departments`.
4. **Leave Management:** PTO and time off tracking via `/rest/v2/time-off/requests`.
5. **Banking & Benefits:** Direct deposit routing and benefits packages.
